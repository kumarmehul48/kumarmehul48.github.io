## 🧠 Knowledge Base — What I Teach & Share

### 📚 On Revenue Cycle Management
> **The 5 Pillars of a Healthy RCM:** Eligibility → Coding → Claim Submission → Denial Management → A/R Follow-up.

> **First-Pass Resolution (FPR) is King:** Every 1% improvement in FPR can boost collections by 2-3%.

> **Denials Are Data, Not Defeats:** Track denial reasons by payer, CPT & provider — patterns reveal where to fix workflows.

### 🤖 On AI Automation
> **Automate the Boring, Humanize the Important:** Let AI handle data entry, follow-ups & reports.

> **Start Small, Scale Smart:** Automate one painful workflow first. Win fast, then expand.

> **AI Won't Replace You — But It Will Replace Your Manual Tasks.**

### 🧩 On No-Code Development
> **Speed Beats Perfection:** A no-code MVP in 2 weeks teaches more than 6 months of planning.

> **The Best Stack Is the One You'll Actually Use:** Bubble + Airtable + Make.com can replace 80% of custom dev.

### 📣 On Business Marketing
> **Positioning > Promotion:** A clear position sells itself.

> **The 3-Layer Funnel:** Attract → Convert → Retain. Most businesses only build the middle.

### ⚡ On Productivity
> **Systems > Willpower:** You don't need more motivation — you need better defaults.

> **Time-Block + Energy-Match:** Do deep work when your energy peaks. Admin when it dips.

### 🧭 On Career & Life Counseling
> **Clarity Comes From Action, Not Thinking.**

> **Every Problem Has 3 Layers:** Surface, story, system. Fix the system.
